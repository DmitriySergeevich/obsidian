Make offers
- will + infinitive  
	I will carry this case - я понесу этот чемодан
- can + infinitive 
	Can I phone Stefan for you? - Могу я позвонить Стефану за вас?
- Do you want me/us + to infinitive
	Do you want me to talk to Laura? - Ты хочешь что бы я поговорил с Лорой?
- Let me + infinitive 
	Let me try and open it - Дай-ка я попробую открыть его
- Shall I + infinitive
	Shall I answer the door? -Мне открыть дверь?

Accept offers
	Yes, please. 
	Thanks, That's realy kind/good of you. 
	That's great helps. 
	That's would be great.
Refusing offers
	I'm fine, thanks. 
	It's okay, thanks. 
	Thank you, but I'm okay.
