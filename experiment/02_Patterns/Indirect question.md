## Core idea
In indirect questions we use statement word order (not question word order).

---
## Yes/no questions
If we add indirect phrase to question with modal verb, we use if or whether to connect the clauses
#### Examples
- Could you tell me if I have to pay for your help?
- Do you know whether I can pay by credit card?

---
## Wh- question 
When we form an indirect wh- question, we use statement word order.

#### Examples
- I'd like to know when the next train leaves.
- Can you tell me what time the gym closes?
---
## Comparison
- Direct: Where is the station?  
- Indirect: Do you know where the station is?

- Direct: Is it free?  
- Indirect: Could you tell me if it is free?
