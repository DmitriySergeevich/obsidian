## Core idea
Some adjectives are formed from nouns by adding suffixes.
They usually describe something that **has the quality of the noun**.

## Pattern noun + -y
- sun -> sunny
- rain -> rainy
- snow -> snowy
- fog -> foggy

## Pattern noun + -ful
- use -> useful
- pain -> painful
