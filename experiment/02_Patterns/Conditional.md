## Core idea
We use this construction if we say about possible or hypothetical situations.

---
## Possible situations
When we says about possible situations in the future and their consequences, we use constructions named **first conditional**
If/when + present simple + will

### Example
If you learn languages you will be able to speak in foreign countries.

---
## Hypothetical situations
When we says about an unreal ot imaginary situations and its consequence we use **second conditional**.
If + past simple + would
### Example 
If I met you earlier we would travel together.
