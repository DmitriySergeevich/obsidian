## Core idea

Describe how an action is done.

---
## Pattern
adjective + ly
If an adjective ends in -y, change y to i and add -ly.

---
## Position
Usually put after a verb.
Don't put between a verb and its object.

---
## Exception
good - well
fast - fast
hard - hard
early - early
late - late

---
## Examples
- She speaks English **fluently**. 
- She speaks English **well**.
- She answered the question **carefully**.
- He spoke **angrily**.

---
## Note
After **verbs of sensation** we use adjectives, not adverbs.
