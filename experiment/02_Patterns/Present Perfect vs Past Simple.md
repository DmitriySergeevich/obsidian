## Core idea
Present Perfect – describes experiences or actions in the past **without specifying the exact time**.

Past Simple – describes completed actions in the past **with a specific time or moment**.

---
## Main difference 

#### Past Simple - main event
- Completed action
- Moves the story forward
- Show sequence of events
---
#### Present Perfect Simple - experience
- No exact time mentioned
- Expresses personal experience
- Shows a result or relevance to the present
- Can refer to an unfinished period up to now
##### Examples:
- I have been to Spain.
- I have studied at the university.
---
## Storytelling function
 Past Simple = main storyline / sequential actions
- I arrived.
- I met my friend.
- We went home.

Present Perfect Simple = background experience or life experience
- I've been to France
- I've played this game
