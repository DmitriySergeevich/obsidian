## Core idea
How time is positioned

---
## Before/After - sequence
Used for order. Using with specific time or event.
#### Examples:
- Before dinner I watched TV.
- After work I will go to the theater.
- We need to be here before 7 p.m.
---
## During - inside in period
Indicates certain period or event when somethings happens
#### Examples:
- I met him during the meeting.
- He called me during dinner.
---
## Until/Till - endpoint
Action continues up to a point.
#### Examples 
- I was doing it until the evening.
- He will work till 5 p.m.
---
## Between - two points
Two exact moments or things. 

Pattern: 
between A and B
#### Examples
- I lived here between 2002 and 2005.
- He gets in between 7 and 9 p.m.
---
## In -> large time units
Years, months, seasons, parts of day. Period of time, when somethings will happen.
#### Examples:
- I went to Moscow **in 2020**.
- I will read a book **in March**.
- I have breakfast **in the morning**.
- The conference finishes **in three hours**.
---
## At - exact time
- Clock time
- Fixed phrases - **weekend, night**

#### Examples:
- I wake up **at 7 a.m.**
- Let's go there **at the weekend**
---
## On - days/date
- days of the week
- full dates
- special days
- specific days with modifiers (that/this/last/next)

#### Examples:
- I work **on Monday**
- We met **on July 5th**
- He was born **on 12 May 1998**
- I stayed home **on my birthday**
