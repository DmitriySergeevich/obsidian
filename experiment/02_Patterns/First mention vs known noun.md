## Core idea
Articles show whether a noun is new or already known to the listener.
a/an = new information  
the = shared / known information

---
## Pattern:
a -> first time
the -> shared / known information

---
## Example:
- I saw **a dog**. **The dog** was beautiful.
- Open **the window**. (we both see it
- Where is **the bathroom**? (usually only one)
- I talked to **the manager**.
- **The teacher** is late today.
