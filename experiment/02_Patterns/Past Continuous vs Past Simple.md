## Core idea
Past Continuous - shows background or ongoing actions in the past 
Past Simple - shows  main finished actions in the past

Past Simple - what happened
Past Continuous - what was happening

---
## Main difference 

#### Past Simple - main event
- Completed action
- Moves the story forward
- Show sequence
##### Example
- I wrote the text.
- I visited a doctor.
---
#### Past Continuous - background

- Action in progress
- Sets the scene
- Describe context
##### Example
- I was watching TV.
- She was sleeping.
---
#### Interrupted Action

Often use together

Past Continuous + when + Past Action

##### Examples
- I was studying when she called.
---
#### Parallel actions

Two actions happening at the same time = Past Continuous
##### Example:
- He **was cooking** while she **was cleaning**.
- They **were talking** and **laughing**.

---
#### Storytelling function
 Past Simple = storyline / main actions
- I arrived.
- I met my friend.
- We went home.
Past Continuous = atmosphere / details
- It was raining.
- People were walking fast.
- Music was playing.
---
#### Time markers
 Past Simple
- yesterday
- last week
- in 2020
- then
- suddenly
Past Continuous
- while
- when
- at that moment
- all day/night
