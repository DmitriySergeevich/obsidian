## Core idea
Used to describe something **full of or characterized by** the noun.
Sometimes the final consonant is doubled before **-y**.

---
## Examples
- sun → sun**ny**
- fun → fun**ny**
- fog → fog**gy**
- rain → rain**y**
- wind → wind**y**
- snow → snow**y**

## Notes
The final consonant is doubled if the noun ending CVC on stress and the last letter not -x or -w

---
## Meaning
- rainy day → a day with rain
- sunny weather → weather with sun
