## Core idea
Don't use articles in this case. No article = not pointing to a concrete thing.

---
## General meaning
Used when we talk about things as a class

#### Examples:
- I like **music**.
- She loves **coffee**

---
## Plural nouns
Plural nouns without article usually mean "all in general".

#### Examples:
- **Students** need motivation.
- **Cars** are expensive now.
- **Books** help you think. 

---
## Abstract nouns
Ideas, feelings, states

#### Examples:
- He needs **confidence**.
- I feel **fear**.

---
## Proper names (most place)
Before 
- cities
- countries(most)
- streets
- lakes
- mountains

Use articles if name of country contains political structure (for example the Russian Federation, the United States of America).

#### Examples:
- I live in **Moscow**.
- We walked along **Baker Street**
- He climbed **Everest**
---
## Fixed expression
Common phrases without articles
- at **home**
- at **work**
- at **school**
- in **bed**
- by **car**
- on **foot**
- go to **church**
- go to **prison** (in general sense)

#### Examples:
- He is **at work** now.
- I go to **school** every day.
---
## Meals, sports, languages

#### Examples:
- We had **breakfast** at 8.
- He plays **football**.
- I study **English**.
- She speaks **Spanish**.
