## Core idea:

Both related to time, but focus differently.

---
## During
indicates certain period or event when somethings happens

**Pattern**: 
during + noun (event/period)

---
## For
Indicates the duration of an action or event. Emphasizing how long something happened.

**Pattern**:
for + period of time

---
## Examples

- I've lived here **for** three years.
- I've been waiting **for** three hours.

- I met him **during** the meeting.
- He called me **during** dinner.
