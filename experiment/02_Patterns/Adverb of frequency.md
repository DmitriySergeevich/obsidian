## Core idea
How often an event happen.

---
## Words
- always
- usually
- often
- sometimes
- not often 
- hardly ever (almost never)
- occasionally (sometimes)
- never
- normally

---
## Position
- before main verb
- after be
- Often, sometimes and occasionally can be at the ending or at the beginning of sentence.

---
## Examples
- I often forget to call my friends.
- I hardly ever drink coffee in the evening.
