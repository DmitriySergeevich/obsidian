## Core idea
We use different patterns to talk about future plans.

It depends on the type of plan or arrangement.

## Plan without arrangement
In this case we have decided to do something, but there is no fixed arrangement. For example - I plan to go a concert, but I haven't bought a ticket.
### Pattern
be + going to

### Examples
- I'm going to start university next year.
- She's going to visit another country.

## Plan with arrangement
In this case, we have a plan and a fixed arrangement (time/place/people). We usually specify a future time such as next week.

### Pattern
Use Present Continuous
be + V-ing

### Examples
- We are going to a restaurant after work. (It means I and other people have arrangement)
- I'm starting my course in September.

## Unsure plan
In this case we are unsure about our plans. Maybe it will happen, but maybe not.

### Pattern
subj + might (not)

### Examples
- I might go out later.
- She might visit you later.

## Make plan at the time of speaking
In this case we didn't have a plan, but we make the decision at the time of speaking. 

### Pattern
subj + will (won't)

### Examples
- I will meet you at the station.
- I'll get you a taxi.
