## Core idea
It's important to finish a conversation politely.
We use different phrases to end conversation.

---
## Positive closing
- Nice to meet you
- Great talking to you
- Nice talking to you

---
## Good wishes
- Have fun
- Have a good day

---
## Give a reason
- I've got to go
- That's my (bus/train)

---
## Closing / goodbye
- See you later
- See you
- Bye
