## Core idea
We use different phrases to start a conversation depending on the situation and the person.

---
## Opening
- Excuse me, ...
- Hi / Hello, ...

---
## Check availability / permission
- Do you mind if I (use this) ?
- Is it OK if I (charge my phone there) ?
- Is anyone (sitting here) ?

---
## Small talk
- Is this your first time (at this event) ?
- How long have you (had your dog) ?
- It's a lovely (morning), isn't it?
- Did you hear about (the news)?
- Did you watch (the movie)?
- Are you doing anything nice (at the weekend)?
- How was your (weekend, holiday)?

---
## Ask for information
- Are you going (to London, to the festival)?
- Do you know if there is (wifi, a cafe near here)?

---
## Introduce
- By the way, I am Dmitriy.
