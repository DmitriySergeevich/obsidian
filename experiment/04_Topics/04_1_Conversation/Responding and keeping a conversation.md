## Core idea
It's important to keep a conversation going.
We use different phrases to respond and continue the conversation.

---
## Giving permission / accepting
- No problem
- Go ahead
- Not at all

---
## Offering / inviting
- Be my guest - you are allowed to do it
- Help yourself - take something freely

---
## Counter questions
- What about you?
- How about you?
- And you?

---
## React / show interest
- Really?
- Oh, nice!
- That sounds interesting.
- I see.
- Right.
- No way!
- That's cool.

---
## Add information / shift topic
- Actually, ...
- By the way, ...
- Also, ...
- I think ...
