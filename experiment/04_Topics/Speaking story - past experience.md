## Core idea
When telling a story about past experience, speakers often start with **Present Perfect** and then move to **Past Simple**, **Past Continuous** or **Past Perfect** to add details.

---
## Pattern
1. **Present Perfect** - introduce experience
2. **Past Simple** - tell main events
3. **Past Continuous** - describe background
4. **Past Perfect** — action completed before another past action

---
## Example:
I've been to France. I went there when I was 18. My mom bought plane tickets, and we stayed in Paris. While we were walking around the city, I was taking photos and listening to street musicians. It was an amazing experience.

---
## Notes
- Use **Past Perfect** sparingly — only when you need to show which past action happened first.  
- Combine **Past Continuous** for background with **Past Simple** for main events.  
- Time expressions help structure the story: by the time, when, while, already, before.
