## Core idea
We use these phrases to explain the reason for something.
Often used when we are late, cancel plans, or apologise.

---
## Softening
Use these phrases to soften your apology or explanation.
- I'm afraid ...
- Sorry, but ...
- The thing is ...

---
## Reasons beyond our control
- There was a delay
- There was a problem with (the train)
- My (train) was delayed
- My (train) got cancelled
- The (traffic/journey) was terrible

---
## Our mistake
- I got the (date/time/place etc) wrong
- I got lost
- I got off at the wrong bus stop
- I got held up at work

---
## Personal reasons
- I had to make an urgent call
- Something came up
- I had to deal with something
