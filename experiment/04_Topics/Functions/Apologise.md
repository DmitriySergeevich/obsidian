## Core idea
Sometimes something was wrong, and we want to apoligise for that. We can use different phrases for that.

---
## For being late
- Sorry I'm late
- Sorry to keep you waiting

--- 
## General apology
- I'm really sorry
- I apologise
- Sorry about that

---
## Promise
- I'll be there as soon as possible
- I'll call you back
