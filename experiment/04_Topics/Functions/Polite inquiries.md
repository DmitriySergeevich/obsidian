## Core idea
We use these phrases to make questions more polite.
They are often used to form indirect questions.

---
## Examples
- **Could you tell me** if I have to pay for your work?
- **Could you explain** how it works?
- **I'd like to know** when you get home.
- **Do you know** where the hotel is?
- **Can I ask** if you know the way?
- **Can you tell me** what time the club closes? 

---
## Note
In indirect questions we use statement word order (not question order). Use if / whether for yes/no questions.
