## Core idea
Articles show how the listener understands the noun.

---
## a/an - new or one of many
Used when the listener does not know the noun yet.
Used for:
- first mention
- one of many
- jobs and roles
#### Example:
- I bought a laptop
- She is a doctor
---
## The
Used when the listener can identify the noun.

Used when:
- mentioned before
- clear from context
- unique
- defined group
- if name of country contains political structure (for example the Russian Federation, the United States of America).
#### Example:
- Open the door
- The sun is bright
---
## Zero article
Used when we do not point to a specific thing.
Used when:
- things in general
- plurals
- most cities
- abstract ideas
#### Example:
- I like music
- Dogs are friendly
