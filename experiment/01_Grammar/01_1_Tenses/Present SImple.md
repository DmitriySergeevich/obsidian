## Core idea
What is always true, usually true, or generally true.
#### Used when
- speak about generally facts
- repeatable events
- routines
- habit

---
## Key patterns
- Positive:
	subject + verb (he/she/it -> verb + s)
- Negative 
	subject + do/does + not + verb (he/she/it -> does)
- Question
	do/does + subject + verb (he/she/it -> does)

---
## Examples
- I wake up at 8 a.m.
- I always call my mom in the evening.
- I hardly ever eat fast food.
