## Core idea
Used for finished actions in the past at a specific time.
Often used for main events in stories.
#### Used when:
- action is finished
- time is known or clear
- sequence of past events

---
## Key patterns 
- positive 
	subject + V2 / V-ed
- negative
	subject + did + not (didn't) + verb
- question
	(wh-word) + did + subject + verb


---
## Example
- **I didn't play** football when I was young.
- **Did she eat** breakfast?
- **Why did she skip** class?
- **She went** to school yesterday
