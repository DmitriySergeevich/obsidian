## Core idea
Used to describe actions or situations that are happening right now or around the current time.  
Also used for temporary situations, future plans, or ongoing trends.
#### Used when:
- Action in progress at the moment of speaking
- Temporary situation (not permanent)
- Future plans or arrangements
- Repeated actions with “always” to show irritation or emphasis

---
## Key patterns 

---
## Example
- I **am sitting** at my desk now.  
- She **is listening** to music while studying.  
- We **are having** dinner at 7 p.m. tonight.  
- They **are always arriving** late to meetings.  
- He **is staying** at his parents’ house this week.
  - He **is always complaining** about the weather.
---
## Notes
- Often appears with time expressions: now, at the moment, today, these days  
- Use for temporary actions, contrast with Present Simple (permanent facts or routines)  
- Can indicate ongoing change or trend: “The climate **is getting** warmer.”
- Don't use with **verbs of sensation**
