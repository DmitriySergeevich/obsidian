## Core idea
Used to describe actions or situations that were in progress at a specific time in the past.  
Often used to give background information or set the scene in a story.
#### Used when:
- action in progress at a certain moment in the past
- action interrupted by another action
- two or more parallel actions happening at the same time
- background details in a story

---
## Key patterns 
- positive 
	subject + was/were + V-ing
- negative
	subject + wasn't/weren't + V-ing
- question
	(wh-word) + was/were + subject + V-ing

you/we/they - were
I/he/she/it - was

---
## Example
- **I was sitting** at home when Alex called me.
- **He was listening** music and reading a book.
- **Was she running** when you watched her?

---
## Notes
- Often appears with time expressions like: while, when, at that moment  
- Used to contrast with Past Simple (action that interrupts, main event)
- Don't use with **verbs of sensation**
