## Core idea
Used to describe an action or situation that was completed **before another action or moment in the past**.  
It shows the **sequence of past events**.
#### Used when:
- To show that one action happened **before another past action**  
- To give background information in a story  
- To show cause and effect in the past  
- With time expressions: by the time, before, after  
---
## Key patterns 
Positive
	subject + had + V3/ V-ed
Negative
	subject + had not (hadn't) + V3/ V-ed
Question
	Had + subject + V3/ V-ed?

---
## Example
- I **had already eaten** when they arrived.  
- She **had never seen** the ocean before that trip.  
- By the time he got to the station, the train **had left**.  
- They were excited because they **had won** the competition.  
- Had you **finished** the report before the meeting?
 ---
## Notes
- Often appears with Past Simple to indicate **which action came first**.  
- Usually used with time markers: by the time, before, after, already, just, yet.  
- Can help clarify sequences in storytelling.
