## Core idea
Past experience connected to now
#### Used when 
- no exact time
- experience
- result now
- unfinished period

---
## Key patterns 
- positive 
	subject + have/has + V3/V+ed
- negative
	subject + have/has + not + V3/V+ed
- question
	question word/empty +  have/has  +  subject + V3/V+ed 

---
## Example:
- I have played this game
- He has going to Spain
